# Learning resources — external video/audio

Curated watch-list for the short-fiction lane. One video at a time,
always paired with the module exercise it feeds — watching is not a rep.
Bangla counterparts get added whenever found.

| Resource | What it is | Serves | Link |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quotidian Writer — *Writing Tips for Beginners* | Short focused craft videos (10–20 min) | Module 1: start with *Anatomy of a Scene* (দৃশ্য বনাম সারমর্ম), then *Show, Don't Tell*, *4 Plotting Pitfalls* | [playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLJctcoQLpndb-uJ32YP4vnl0nHkgi_QsL) |
| Brandon Sanderson — BYU Creative Writing (2020) | Full university semester on YouTube; SFF-framed, universal craft | নকশা/structure depth — Lecture 2 (plotting) and structure lecture; watch selectively, not front-to-back | [playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLSH_xM-KC3Zv-79sVZTTj-YA6IAqh8qeQ) |
| Flash Fiction 101 & Freewrite 500 | Flash-fiction primer + six-word-story warm-up | Module 2 (drabble week) — the "art of omission" framing | [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=obINCqSqBnA) |
| Christopher Fielden — free short story course | 5 short videos, beginner-focused, tips + common mistakes | Optional alongside Module 2–3; publication-oriented, take what transfers | [course](https://learn.christopherfielden.com/p/free-short-story-writing-course) |

Rules of use:
- Watch *because* a module needs it — never binge ahead of the writing.
- After watching, one line of takeaway goes into the active change folder
  or the device catalog (if it names a technique).
- Found something better → replace the row, note why.
