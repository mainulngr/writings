# Production — how a children's piece becomes a published book

Pointer mark. The writing happens here; the product, rights, art and audio
machinery live in the **onkurreads** repo
(`/home/akm/projects/hubs/onkurlabs/onkurreads`).

## The boundary

- **`writings/` — authorship.** Originals only. No derivative text, no
  licence records, no source records. A children's story written here is
  owned outright.
- **`onkurreads/` — product.** Adaptations, translations, rights, credits,
  art, audio, publishing. Derivative work never enters this repo.

## What this means for the children's lane here

- A story written in `stories/children/` can be promoted into the shelf as a
  Tier C **original** — no licence, no credit chain, no derivative question.
- Two original slots are held for exactly that.
- The rewrite lane in onkurreads is the *craft* counterweight: it trains
  register and read-aloud rhythm, but hands you the plot. Structure practice
  stays here, in the short-fiction course.
- `voice-check` (children band) runs on rewrites too — the audit is shared
  even though the record is not.

## Hard facts worth remembering

- All 40 Storybooks Bangladesh items are **CC BY-NC** → unusable in a paid
  app. Pratham/StoryWeaver is the only Bengali pool that is both literary and
  commercially clear.
- Bangla TTS ceiling today ≈ 4.46 MOS vs 4.90 natural speech; accent (bn-BD
  vs bn-IN) is the bigger failure than naturalness.
- AI art is not copyrightable; flagship originals want a human illustrator.
