## Form taxonomy (the craft layer)

six-word (exactly 6) → twitterature (<280c) → dribble/minisaga (~50) →
55-fiction (exactly 55) → drabble (exactly 100) → microfiction (<300–500)
→ sudden fiction (<750) → flash (<1,000–1,500) → short story (1,000+).

Craft invariants across all: complete arc in miniature; elements may live
in the unsaid (implication over statement); scene over summary; every word
pulls double duty.

## The opening

Gentle, reflective, *literary-quality* Bangla micro-fiction with visual
presentation is thinly occupied. Bangla web fiction skews romance/horror/
erotica mass genres; the FB ecosystem is mostly raw text posts. The
intersection — his voice + micro-form + styled presentation — is the
whitespace.

## The graveyard (what fails)

- Generic AI-flavored pap with no observed detail.
- Moral-of-the-story preachiness (his avoids list already forbids).
- Walls of text with no visual anchor or hook in line one.
- Serial abandonment — series that stop mid-arc kill followership.

## Verdict

Continue — the lane is real, the whitespace is genuine, and his existing
styled-variant habit already lives at the micro-fiction boundary. The
course must teach structure, not just mood — mood he already has.

## Recalibration (2026-10-15)
