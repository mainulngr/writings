# Voice Check — audit procedure

`_marks/voice.md` is the source of truth. This checklist is the audit's
checkpoints — when the two disagree, the mark wins.

## Pass 1 — register (all content)

- **Subject choice** — মানুষ / লোকজন / আমরা, not finger-pointing আমি/তুমি
  (except deliberate Human Version / first-person pieces).
- **Hedged claims** — observations, not verdicts: মনে হয়, হয়তো, অনেকের
  ক্ষেত্রে, দেখা যায়.
- **Landing** — ends soft: hopeful note or innocent punchline. Never a
  burden.
- **The avoids list** — no personal attacks, no preachy imperatives, no
  cynical hopelessness, no attention-seeking drama, no "তোমরা সবাই"
  instructions.

## Pass 2 — channel fit

- **social/** — slot structure present (Main caption + styled-bg variants);
  variants genuinely 1–4 lines and self-contained.
- **stories/, books/** — band fit per `_marks/audience-bands.md`: word
  count in budget, vocabulary right for the reader, read-aloud quality for
  children.
- **blog/** — observes rather than instructs; ends on resonance.

## Output

- Short report: ✓/✗ per checkpoint with line references, then minimal
  suggested edits — smallest change that fixes the violation.
- Do not rewrite whole passages unless asked; Mainul keeps his sentences.

## Critique mode — the editor's memo

A craft-level pass, distinct from the voice audit — for stories and longer
pieces. Output in English; the piece stays untouched (no edits). Run the
voice audit first, then this.

1. **Improve** — what's working but under-delivered (structure, pacing,
   character, ending); the smallest change that unlocks it.
2. **Discard** — what weakens the piece: redundant beats, explained
   emotions, dead scenes. Cite lines.
3. **Add** — what's missing: a beat, a sensory anchor, a turn, a breath.
4. **Honest rating** — a real number (/10) with the evidence. No flattery.
5. **Engagement read** — attention mechanics only (hook strength, loops,
   payoff). Each suggestion carries a **voice-cost** label: what the lift
   would trade away in register. Declining the lift is a valid verdict.

Never rewrite — Mainul curates the final prose; this is the editor's memo.

## Feeding the voice

- When Mainul edits a draft after your audit, distill the edit: what changed
  and what rule does it imply? The *second* time the same correction
  appears, propose it as a `_marks/voice.md` edit — offer it, never apply
  silently.
